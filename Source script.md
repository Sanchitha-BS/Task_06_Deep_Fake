# Source Script

**Title:** A Coach's Perspective: End of Season Reflection

This script is adapted from the Task 5 narrative, a coach-advisory answer built on ground-truth season stats, including a comparison of LLM-generated recommendations against self-verified analysis.

---

Molly Guzik carried this team, 44 goals, 10 assists, best draw control rate on the roster.
If one player decided our season, it's her.

But the numbers made me look harder at the rest. Izzy Lahah barely scored but led the team
in ground balls and caused turnovers, unglamorous work that never makes a highlight reel.
Caroline Trinkaus quietly had the second-best all-around season, and I don't think I gave
her enough credit.

The numbers also challenged my assumptions. I'd have said defense was our weak spot; it's
actually our stronger unit, offense is where the real gap is. And my instinct was to keep
feeding Molly more touches next year. The math said otherwise, she's already near her
ceiling, and the real upside is in the middle tier: Trinkaus, Rich, Volpe.

I also used a couple of AI tools this season. One gave a confident, wrong answer that
would've misdirected our development plan. The other got it right, but only after I made
it show its work. Lesson: useful for spotting patterns, not something I trust blind on a
roster decision.

Plan for next season: keep Molly doing what she does, get the middle tier more trust and
touches, and keep verifying the numbers myself before I believe anyone's answer.
